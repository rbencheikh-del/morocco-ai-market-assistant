# Compliance Notes

This is not legal advice. Treat this as an early checklist for qualified review.

## Guardrails

- Present outputs as educational research and decision support.
- Require suitability profile before showing personalized portfolio analysis.
- Keep every AI signal auditable with input data, model version, timestamp, and output.
- Separate research and paper trading from live execution.
- Avoid wording that implies guaranteed returns or licensed financial advice.
- Show source timestamps for all market data.

## Morocco-Specific Workstream

- Confirm applicable requirements with Moroccan financial-market counsel.
- Review expectations from relevant market authorities before collecting deposits, routing orders, or presenting investment recommendations.
- Validate licensing constraints for Casablanca Stock Exchange market data.
- Define broker partnership responsibilities before live trading is considered.

## Production Controls

- KYC and identity verification if the app moves beyond education.
- AML and sanctions screening where applicable.
- Encryption for user portfolio and identity data.
- Role-based internal admin access.
- Incident response plan and data retention policy.

# MVP Plan

## Product Positioning

morocco-ai-market-assistant should launch first as an educational research and analytics app for Moroccan retail investors. Live order routing should wait until market data licensing, broker partnerships, compliance review, and security controls are in place.

## First Release

- Account creation and login.
- Suitability questionnaire and risk score.
- Casablanca Stock Exchange watchlist.
- Market data ingestion pipeline with source timestamps.
- Paper portfolio with MAD cash and simulated holdings.
- Explainable AI signal summaries with audit records.
- Alerts for risk concentration, unusual volume, and major price moves.
- French and English UI, with Arabic planned after product copy stabilizes.

## AI Responsibilities

- Explain screening logic in plain language.
- Identify risk drivers and missing data.
- Provide portfolio education and scenario analysis.
- Refuse to present certainty, guaranteed returns, or personalized financial advice.

## AI Non-Responsibilities

- No autonomous trading.
- No direct order placement in the MVP.
- No guarantee of returns.
- No replacement for licensed financial advice.

## Launch Criteria

- Real market data source approved and timestamped.
- User risk profile stored and used in every signal request.
- Audit log for every generated signal.
- Product disclaimers visible before users can rely on outputs.
- Security review for authentication, account data, and portfolio data.
